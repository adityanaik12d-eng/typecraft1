Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how TypeCraft ("we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences and improve your experience.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies: Required for the site to function. These store your theme, font size, and typing settings. Cannot be disabled.

Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use TypeCraft so we can improve it. We use anonymized data only.

Preference Cookies: Remember your language selection, game scores, and typing history between sessions.

Third-Party Cookies

If you have enabled advertising, Google AdSense may place cookies to show relevant ads. Amazon Associates may also use cookies to track affiliate link clicks.

Your Rights

You can accept or reject non-essential cookies at any time using the banner at the bottom of the page. Rejecting cookies will not affect core functionality but your preferences will not be saved between sessions.
